Acme Corp Employee Handbook

Vacation Policy
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per year. Vacation days accrue monthly. Unused days can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal.

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Everyone must be in the office on Tuesdays for the team meeting. Remote workers are given a $300 one-time allowance for home office equipment.

Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days with receipts attached. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $60 per day. Flights must be booked through the company travel portal.

Sick Leave
Employees get 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
